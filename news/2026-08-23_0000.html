C1How Claude's Text Watermark Works — Anthropic Implements SynthID-Text for EU AI Act Compliance

📰 Article (English)

Anthropic has published a detailed technical explanation of how Claude's new text watermarking system works. Implemented to comply with the EU AI Act, the watermark operates by subtly altering the source of randomness Claude uses when selecting between equivalent word choices during text generation. The method is based on Scott Aaronson's 2022 cryptographic watermarking proposal and uses Google DeepMind's SynthID-Text technique as its foundation. The watermark produces no visible changes to outputs, requires no additional tokens, and carries zero performance overhead. Crucially, the watermark cannot be traced back to individual users or organizations — it only identifies Claude as the author. Internal testing shows no degradation in output quality or readability. The system also applies C2PA provenance metadata to file outputs like PDFs and images. Limitations are acknowledged: the watermark is less effective on highly constrained factual outputs (where Claude has limited word choice), on short text samples under ~800 tokens, and cannot distinguish human-rewritten AI content. A watermark detection API is coming soon.
