📰 本文 + 和越対訳

Anthropicが公開したClaude Opus 5向けの公式プロンプティングガイドの内容を分析した記事が、エンジニアの間で大きな話題となっている。最大のポイントは、これまでのOpus 4.8などで有効だったプロンプトのテクニックが、Opus 5では逆効果になるケースがあるという点だ。具体的には「結果を検証してください」「再確認してください」といった指示がOpus 5では自動的に実行されるため、重ねて書くと処理コストが膨らむだけとなる。また、応答の長さについてはeffortパラメータだけでは制御できず、「簡潔に」「短く答えて」という明示的なテキスト指示が必要になる。思考機能はデフォルトでオンのまま使い、effortの起点は従来のxhighから`high`に下げることが推奨されている。さらにサブエージェントへの過剰な委任を防ぐため、エージェント利用の明示的な制限を追加する必要もあるという。Opus 5はFable 5に迫る性能を半額以下で提供するため、実用的なチューニングのポイントが開発者に強く求められている。

Anthropic đã công bố hướng dẫn prompting chính thức cho Claude Opus 5, và nội dung của nó đang gây chấn động trong cộng đồng kỹ sư AI. Điểm quan trọng nhất là nhiều kỹ thuật prompt hiệu quả với Opus 4.8 lại phản tác dụng với Opus 5. Các lệnh "hãy xác minh lại" hay "kiểm tra lại kết quả" bây giờ là thừa vì model tự động thực hiện bước đó, làm tăng chi phí không cần thiết. DX (Developer Experience) has published research introducing the DX Core 4, a developer productivity measurement framework that extends beyond the widely-used DORA metrics. The four dimensions of DX Core 4 are: Speed (how fast software moves from idea to production), Effectiveness (whether engineers can work without friction), Quality (defect rates, reliability, maintainability), and Impact (whether the work delivers real business value). The framework is designed to address a key gap in DORA: DORA metrics focus on deployment pipeline performance but don't capture developer experience or business impact. DX Core 4 integrates survey-based signals (developer sentiment, friction points) with objective system metrics to give a fuller picture. Notably, it also provides guidance on how AI tooling adoption should be measured — not just through usage rates but through impact on the four dimensions. As teams increasingly adopt Claude Code, Copilot, and similar tools, having a principled framework for evaluating their ROI is critical. The research draws on data from over 1,000 engineering teams and is freely available.
