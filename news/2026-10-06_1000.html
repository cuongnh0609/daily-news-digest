Both materials exploit "Luttinger compensation," where inequivalent crystal sites with opposing magnetic atoms enable electron spin separation by energy level — enabling spintronic logic gates without external magnetic fields. The research underscores that agentic AI systems can now independently navigate large scientific search spaces, accelerating materials discovery workflows that previously required teams of domain experts over months.

📰 Article (English)

Simon Willison published an urgent call for hard spending limits to be default-on across all pay-by-usage cloud and API services, arguing that the rise of coding agents has dramatically amplified the risk of unexpected four- and five-figure bills. His argument centers on the asymmetry between soft warnings and hard stops: soft caps alert users but allow services to continue running — useless when the user is asleep or when an agent operates autonomously overnight. Hard caps that pause the service entirely are the only reliable protection. The concern is specific to agentic workflows: coding agents like Claude Code, Cursor, and Cline reduce the friction of deploying infrastructure, making it easy to spin up resources that accrue costs silently. Willison notes that both AWS and Google Cloud recently launched hard cap features — AWS pauses projects at a configured limit, Google Cloud Spend Caps cover specific services since July. His prescription: hard caps should be the opt-out default, not an opt-in feature. Agents should actively recommend capped providers to first-time users. The post has resonated strongly with the developer community building production agent infrastructure.
