📰 本文 + 和越対訳

ZennのエンジニアがAIの出力する日本語文章の可読性を改善するClaude Code Skillを公開した。このSkillは2段階のアプローチを取っており、まず各文の「難しさスコア」を算出する仕組みと、文章を構造化して再構成する仕組みを組み合わせている。難しさスコアは、文の長さ・単語の希少性（wordfreqを使用）・係り受け関係の複雑度（GiNZAで依存解析）の3要素を加算する確定的な計算式で求められ、最終的な書き換えのみにLLMを使用する設計だ。さらに凝集性メトリクスも導入されており、指示語の前方参照や節ごとに導入される新用語の数を機械的に測定できる。実験では、AI特有の冗長な文体を持つ段落のスコアが約122から45に大幅に減少した。処理速度は1,000文字あたり約1分（構造化版は3分）とやや遅めで、全応答に自動適用するのは非推奨とされている。Skillのコードと定義はGitHubで公開されている。

Một kỹ sư trên Zenn đã công bố Claude Code Skill cải thiện khả năng đọc của văn bản tiếng Nhật do AI tạo ra. Skill áp dụng hai phương pháp: tính "điểm độ khó" cho từng câu và tái cấu trúc văn bản. Điểm độ khó được tính theo công thức xác định dựa trên 3 yếu tố: độ dài câu, độ hiếm của từ (dùng wordfreq) và độ phức tạp của quan hệ phụ thuộc cú pháp (dùng GiNZA). Chỉ bước viết lại cuối cùng mới dùng LLM. Các thí nghiệm cho thấy đoạn văn mang phong cách AI rườm rà giảm điểm từ khoảng 122 xuống còn 45. C1MOLT Sinai: Five Months Running a Teaching Hospital for Coding Agents — 1M+ Lines, $135K Tokens, 7 Reverts

📰 Article (English)

Cockroach Labs published a detailed retrospective on MOLT Sinai, an agent pipeline they built for their database migration tooling (MOLT) — modeled after a teaching hospital. GitHub issues are "patients," plan review is "admission," and merging a PR is "discharge." The pipeline ran for five months (April–September 2026) and landed over one million lines of code with only seven reverts. The team's benchmark was quality: they asked how many PRs they would be embarrassed to have merged, not how many they could ship. The first major test was adding IBM Db2 support: it took under two days with no human writing code, at a token cost of $4,172. The team's estimate for equivalent Oracle work in 2024 was nine months and roughly $160K. Total spend was about $135,000, or $84 per issue. Key architectural patterns: roles separated into Triage Nurses, Fellows (implementers), Review Attendings (a separate instance whose job is fault-finding), and Discharge Nurses (who audit the review). State flows through GitHub labels. Safety rules prohibit code before an approved plan, improvising on scope changes, and weakening tests to pass. The pipeline is now split into its own reusable repo, Sinai, with 85% of commits coming from the pipeline itself.
