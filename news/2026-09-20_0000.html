📰 Article (English)

Convai Innovations has released Laya, an open-source System 1 decision engine that delivers structured AI predictions in under 35 milliseconds. Unlike traditional autoregressive LLMs that generate freeform text, Laya evaluates only three decision primitives: choice selection (pick one from N options), ordinal scoring (rate on a scale), and boolean questions (yes/no). For each, it returns calibrated probability distributions rather than a single answer, eliminating hallucination risk by design. Laya is positioned as a direct challenge to TypeSafe AI's Jev — a commercial non-autoregressive model launched in September 2026 to considerable fanfare. The comparison is notable: Laya completes inference in 33ms versus Jev's 236ms (7.8× faster), supports over 100 languages through intelligent routing, and is available as open weights under the Apache 2.0 license at zero cost versus Jev's $0.042 per million tokens. The targeted use cases are high-volume classification tasks: email triage, support ticket routing, phishing detection, content moderation, and real-time fraud scoring. The team argues these workloads don't need language generation at all — and that forcing them through an autoregressive decoder wastes both compute and latency budget. Three specialized checkpoints are provided, optimized for latency, balanced performance, and maximum accuracy respectively. Source code, model weights, and training methodology are published on GitHub under Apache 2.0.
