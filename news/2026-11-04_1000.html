📰 本文 + 和越対訳

Nhóm còn xây dựng Claude Skill tự động kiểm tra ảnh hưởng E2E theo từng PR, dùng GitHub compare API để phát hiện thay đổi selector.

LangChain's engineering team published a detailed benchmark comparing seven browser toolsets for their Managed Deep Agents platform. The winner was Stagehand (from Browserbase), which achieved a 90% pass rate on WebVoyager at $1.98 total cost — versus Playwright MCP's 78% pass rate at $26.71, roughly 13x more expensive and 3x slower per task. Stagehand's key advantage: three purpose-built tools (snapshot for the accessibility tree with element IDs, run for batched actions, and screenshot) rather than granular CDP calls. The team also fixed a major latency issue: agents used fixed waitForTimeout(8000) sleeps even when pages settled in 0.6 seconds. Replacing fixed waits with dynamic idle detection (wait until 500ms of quiet, capped at requested duration) dropped median agent time from 182s to 124s on HardBench with no accuracy loss. The post's core lesson is that tool design drives most of the performance delta, and iterating with evals is the right loop — not intuition.
