C1How Claude's Text Watermark Works — Anthropic Implements SynthID for EU AI Act Compliance

📰 Article + Impact

Anthropic has published a detailed technical explanation of how Claude's text watermarking system works, implemented in compliance with the EU AI Act's transparency requirements. The system is based on SynthID-Text, a method co-developed with Google DeepMind and originally proposed by Scott Aaronson in 2022. Rather than inserting hidden characters or altering visible content, the watermark works by subtly influencing which word Claude selects when multiple equally valid options exist — for instance, choosing "overcast" over "grey" in a particular context. The watermark key controls this randomness at generation time, leaving a statistically detectable pattern across longer text. The approach adds no extra tokens, carries no user-identifying information, and does not affect inference speed or cost. It is less effective on short texts, factual passages, and code where exact output is required. Anthropic is among approximately 190 signatories of the EU Code of Practice on AI-Generated Content Transparency. A detection API will be made available soon, allowing organizations to verify whether Claude was involved in producing a given piece of text.
