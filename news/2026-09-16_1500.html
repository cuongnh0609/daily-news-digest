B-01AIエージェントの「フレームワーク」と「ハーネス」はどう違うのか――LangGraph vs OpenHands に見る設計哲学の分岐点

📰 本文 + 和越対訳

2026年現在、AIエージェント構築には大きく二つの設計思想が存在する。「フレームワーク型」（LangGraph、Pydantic AI）はコードで状態遷移やツール呼び出しを明示的に定義する手法で、監査可能性と予測可能性に優れる反面、柔軟性に欠ける。一方「ハーネス型」（OpenHands、Goose）はモデル自身に意思決定を委ね、その周囲をインフラで囲む構造で、オープンエンドなタスクへの対応力は高いが、デバッグが難しい。著者は選択基準として、コンプライアンス要件や手順の明確なタスクにはフレームワーク型、探索的で手順が不定のタスクにはハーネス型を推奨する。また、プロトタイピングにはCrewAIで全体構造を確認し、本番移行時に重要パスのみLangGraphへ移行するという段階的アプローチを提案している。Gartnerは2026年までにエンタープライズの40%がエージェントを導入すると予測するが、同数の40%がプロジェクト中断に陥るという見立ても示しており、「何を自動化しないか」の判断が今後の差別化要因になると結論付けている。

Năm 2026, có hai triết lý thiết kế chính trong xây dựng AI agent. Cách tiếp cận "framework" (LangGraph, Pydantic AI) định nghĩa tường minh các chuyển đổi trạng thái và lời gọi tool bằng code, vượt trội về khả năng audit và dự đoán nhưng thiếu linh hoạt. Cách tiếp cận "harness" (OpenHands, Goose) ủy thác quyết định cho model và bao quanh nó bằng infrastructure, xử lý tốt các task mở nhưng khó debug hơn. Tác giả khuyến nghị: dùng framework cho task có quy trình rõ ràng và yêu cầu compliance, dùng harness cho task khám phá với bước không xác định. C-01The AI Inference Revolution Is Here — Memory Bandwidth Becomes the New Compute Bottleneck

📰 Article (English)

The AI industry's center of gravity has decisively shifted from model training to inference — using trained models to generate output in production. This transition is driven by increasingly capable LLMs and the explosive growth of reasoning models and autonomous agents that run inference continuously across long multi-step tasks. The fundamental challenge is that inference workloads have different bottlenecks than training: while training saturates compute, inference's decode phase — where tokens are generated one at a time — is starved for memory bandwidth. Current GPU deployments sit idle 50–80% of the time waiting for weights to transfer from DRAM to compute cores. A new wave of hardware startups is attacking this from multiple angles. d-Matrix stacks compute directly onto DRAM to eliminate the bandwidth bottleneck at the chip level. Majestic Labs extends memory interfaces to support commodity DRAM at scale, reducing cost. Groq doubles down on on-chip SRAM to minimize latency. Meanwhile, major cloud providers are adopting hybrid architectures: Nvidia pairs Vera Rubin GPUs with Groq 3 LPUs, while Amazon combines Trainium accelerators with Cerebras wafer-scale engines. Quantization and speculative decoding techniques are also maturing, enabling more tokens per second at lower power budgets. The article argues that inference hardware innovation will be the defining infrastructure story of the next three years.

C-02GPT-6 Astra Builds Running Routes End-to-End — Simon Willison Probes the Agentic Gap Between Capability and Transparency

📰 Article (English)

Simon Willison documented an experiment using ChatGPT Work with GPT-6 Astra to generate personalized running routes from a home address. He requested both 5K and 10K looping routes, and the agentic system delivered within 27 minutes: visualized interactive maps rendered via D3.js and downloadable GPX/GeoJSON files — a complete end-to-end workflow requiring geographic data retrieval, route calculation, and visualization. Behind the scenes, the agent used Nominatim to geocode the address and Overpass to download local OpenStreetMap roads and trails, then calculated routes locally. He argues this is a systemic problem: LLM systems that use context compaction should preserve original tool outputs and make them retrievable via explicit tool calls. As agents take on longer, more consequential tasks, the inability to audit their reasoning and intermediate steps becomes a genuine safety and correctness concern — not just a debugging inconvenience.
