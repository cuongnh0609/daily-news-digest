A-03J:COMネット障害、原因は「外部からの大量アクセス」——約408万世帯に最大9時間影響

📰 本文（日本語）+ 🇻🇳 Bản dịch

ケーブルテレビ大手J:COMは、9月23日に発生したインターネット接続サービスの大規模障害について、原因は「外部からの大量アクセス」によってDNSサーバに高い負荷がかかったためと発表した。障害は約9時間続き、北海道と九州を除く全国エリアの最大約408万世帯に影響が及んだ。同社はサービス復旧後、障害情報の公開が遅れたことを謝罪し、今後はアクセス集中への対策強化とリアルタイム情報提供の改善を進めると説明した。「外部からの大量アクセス」とはDDoS攻撃に類似した現象を指すとみられ、インターネット基盤の脆弱性に対する注目が改めて高まっている。今回の事例は、DNS障害がいかに大規模な接続障害に波及するかを示す典型例となった。

Công ty truyền hình cáp lớn J:COM thông báo nguyên nhân gây ra sự cố gián đoạn dịch vụ internet ngày 23 tháng 9 là do "lượng truy cập lớn từ bên ngoài" tạo ra tải trọng cao trên máy chủ DNS. Sự cố kéo dài khoảng 9 tiếng và ảnh hưởng đến tối đa khoảng 4,08 triệu hộ gia đình trên toàn quốc (trừ Hokkaido và Kyushu). Sau khi khôi phục dịch vụ, công ty đã xin lỗi vì chậm trễ trong việc công bố thông tin sự cố và cho biết sẽ tăng cường các biện pháp chống tập trung truy cập và cải thiện cung cấp thông tin theo thời gian thực. "Lượng truy cập lớn từ bên ngoài" được cho là hiện tượng tương tự tấn công DDoS, làm dấy lên sự chú ý đối với lỗ hổng trong cơ sở hạ tầng internet. Sự việc lần này trở thành ví dụ điển hình cho thấy sự cố DNS có thể lan rộng thành gián đoạn kết nối quy mô lớn như thế nào.

C-01GitHub Releases Proof of Presence — Enterprise Security Gate for High-Impact Actions

📰 Article (English)

GitHub has released Proof of Presence, a new enterprise security feature for GitHub Enterprise Cloud that requires interactive re-authentication before any "high-impact action" can be completed. Available in public preview for managed user enterprises using Microsoft Entra ID, the feature addresses a critical class of supply chain attack where compromised session tokens or stolen cookies are used to take over authenticated sessions. Instead of relying on an existing session, the system redirects users to their identity provider (Entra ID) for fresh authentication — either a re-login or a new MFA challenge — before allowing restricted actions to proceed. High-impact actions covered include: creating personal access tokens, editing webhooks, changing organization security settings, and viewing account recovery codes. The security rationale is to confirm "a real, authorized person is acting at the moment the high-impact action happens," not just that a valid session exists. This matters especially in CI/CD pipelines where long-lived tokens are common and session hijacking is a realistic attack vector. After completing a Proof of Presence challenge, users can continue performing restricted actions for two hours within the same browser session without additional prompts. Pull request merge protection requiring Proof of Presence is announced as coming soon.

C-03Google Project Suncatcher: AI Datacenter in Orbit — First Prototype Satellite to Launch on Falcon 9

📰 Article (English)

Google has announced Project Suncatcher, an ambitious initiative to build AI datacenters in low Earth orbit (LEO), and revealed that the first prototype satellite will launch aboard a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket. The project exploits a key advantage of LEO satellites: they receive near-continuous solar illumination, enabling up to 8x the solar power generation achievable at any ground-based location. The prototype satellite carries TPU (Tensor Processing Unit) accelerators and is co-developed with Planet Labs to validate that the hardware can withstand launch stresses (up to 10G acceleration, 50-100G at the chip level), space radiation, and extreme thermal cycling. The primary unsolved engineering challenge is cooling: in vacuum, convection is impossible, so heat dissipation requires a novel combination of heat pipes and deployable radiators. The long-term vision is a constellation of satellites communicating via high-bandwidth laser links, forming a distributed orbital AI compute cluster. Commercial deployment is targeted for 2027 with two satellites. If successful, Project Suncatcher would represent a fundamental shift in AI infrastructure — moving compute off-planet to access abundant solar energy, potentially reducing the massive land and power footprint of ground AI datacenters.
