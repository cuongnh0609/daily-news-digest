Ngày 27/8, Anthropic công bố tiêu chuẩn chung "MHS (Model Hardware Standard)" cho phép AI agent điều khiển an toàn và hiệu quả các thiết bị phần cứng vật lý. Software engineer Ernest Bednarczyk argues that LLMs consistently underperform in legacy codebases because they lack understanding of the system's existing vocabulary and architectural patterns. His proposed solution, Domain-Driven Agents, combines Domain-Driven Design (DDD) principles with a structured agent workflow to bridge this gap. The core idea splits development into two roles: humans handle strategic decisions (analyzing what changes are needed and where they belong within bounded contexts), while AI handles tactical execution (implementing those decisions in code). The result: agents make fewer incorrect assumptions about where to put code, dramatically reducing the hallucination-driven errors common in brownfield AI-assisted development.
