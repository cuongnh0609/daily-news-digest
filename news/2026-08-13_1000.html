C-01Tailscale Traces Six Months of Database Corruption to a 16-Year-Old SQLite WAL-Reset Bug

📰 Article (English)

Tailscale published a detailed post-mortem on six months of intermittent database corruption in their control plane, ultimately traced to a race condition that had been latent in SQLite for approximately sixteen years. The defect — dubbed the "WAL-Reset bug" — occurs when a write transaction collides with a checkpoint operation during Write-Ahead Logging: pages get marked as flushed to the main database file without the actual write completing, silently corrupting data. Tailscale's non-standard usage pattern, which involved aggressively managing checkpoints manually, made them statistically far more likely to trigger this extremely rare race condition than typical SQLite users. Nineteen separate corruption incidents caused repeated outages across customer tailnets, eroding trust even though no encryption keys or credentials were ever compromised. Debugging required SQLite's core team to build a custom filesystem shim (tmstmpvfs) to log every I/O operation and identify the precise collision sequence. SQLite 3.51.3 ships an additional guard in the checkpointing function to detect when the WAL file has been reset by a concurrent writer. Tailscale confirmed the fix two months post-deployment when monitoring intercepted — and successfully blocked — what would have been the twentieth corruption event.

Elo rating /ˈiːloʊ ˈreɪtɪŋ/ Hệ thống xếp hạng dựa trên kết quả so sánh trực tiếp (head-to-head) — phổ biến trong chess và nay dùng để xếp hạng AI models trên các benchmark.
Long-horizon task /lɒŋ həˈraɪzən tɑːsk/ Tác vụ đòi hỏi nhiều bước suy luận liên tiếp qua nhiều lượt trao đổi (multi-turn, multi-step) — thử thách chính của các agentic model hiện tại.
