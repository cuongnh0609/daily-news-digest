The author concludes these patterns do not clearly match sandbagging but acknowledges limitations: single model, possible evaluation-awareness, and the checker's comments referencing the study's purpose.

As a remediation tool, the author released gguf-radar (MIT license) which scans model files for suspicious templates and hidden bytes without executing them.
