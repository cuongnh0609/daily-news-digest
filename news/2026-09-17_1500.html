株式会社アサインのエンジニア・白井氏がZennに公開した記事が注目を集めている。対面会議ではmacOSのボイスメモ、オンライン会議ではGoogle MeetやZoomの音声を自動で録音し、文字起こしツール「yap」で変換後、Claudeが議事録として整形・保存先まで自動判定するパイプラインだ。技術的な核心は、プロセスタップを使って相手の音声を直接キャプチャし、集約デバイスで自分と相手の音声を同期録音する点にある。トリガーはlaunchdで管理され、「ボイスメモの完了」または「会議アプリのマイク使用開始/終了」を検知して自動で起動する。会議終了から1〜2分で議事録が完成するため、エンジニアが議事録作成に費やす時間をほぼゼロにすることに成功した。

Biên bản hoàn thành trong 1-2 phút sau khi cuộc họp kết thúc, gần như đưa thời gian viết biên bản của kỹ sư về zero.

A new research paper posted on arXiv proposes a technique to push ternary large language model quantization below the theoretical 1.58-bit floor that has been widely cited as the minimum bit-width for preserving model quality. The work demonstrates that it is possible to go even further in weight compression by restructuring the weight distribution used during training, rather than solely applying post-training quantization. Ternary quantization represents model weights using only three discrete values (typically -1, 0, and +1), enabling extreme compression that dramatically reduces memory footprint and accelerates inference on hardware without floating-point units. The 1.58-bit figure (log₂(3)) has been treated as a fundamental ceiling because each weight can carry at most log₂(3) bits of information in a ternary representation. The paper's core insight is that by introducing structured sparsity during training — where a controlled fraction of weights are forced to zero before quantization — the effective information density per weight exceeds what a naive ternary scheme would allow, without requiring a larger codebook. Benchmark results show competitive performance on standard NLP tasks with significantly reduced memory overhead. For engineers building inference pipelines for embedded devices or edge hardware, this represents a meaningful step toward sub-2-bit models that retain usable quality.
