B2スペック駆動開発とcc-sddの仕組みを図解 — requirements・design・tasksの3文書ループとAIエージェント承認ゲート

The entry notes that the broader category of spec-driven development tools (including Spec Kit and Kiro) is growing rapidly as organizations move AI coding from experiments to production workflows.
