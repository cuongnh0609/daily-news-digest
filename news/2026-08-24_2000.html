📰 Article (English)

Kaspersky researchers have documented the first known Android malware campaign specifically targeting automotive head unit systems — the Android-based infotainment computers built into modern vehicles. The malware, attributed to the MoYu Group behind the BADBOX botnet platform, exploits the legitimate firmware update mechanism of DoFun head units to install unauthorized APK payloads. The infection follows a three-stage chain. A JarService dropper uses XOR-encrypted payloads loaded via reflection to avoid detection. The same "zhima" proxy module was previously seen in BADBOX-compromised TV set-top boxes, suggesting the threat actor is expanding to any always-on Android device with network access. Kaspersky detects the threat under HEUR:Trojan-Proxy.AndroidOS.Zhima.* and related signatures.
