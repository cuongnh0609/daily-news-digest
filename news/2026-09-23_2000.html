B-01VS CodeとCursorを並行起動——分離できていない並列AI開発の落とし穴と実践知

GitHub Copilot has added Claude Opus 5.5 to its model roster, making Anthropic's latest and most capable model available across all Copilot subscription tiers — Individual, Business, and Enterprise. Claude Opus 5.5 brings notable improvements for Copilot workflows: 40% lower cost compared to Claude Opus 5, approximately 30% faster response times, and significantly enhanced performance on agentic coding benchmarks including SWE-bench and internal GitHub evaluations. This availability reflects GitHub's strategy of providing model choice rather than locking users into a single provider, following the earlier additions of GPT-6 Sol/Luna and Grok 4.7 to the same platform within the past week.
