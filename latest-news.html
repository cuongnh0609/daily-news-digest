📰 本文 + 和越対訳

Zennに投稿された技術記事は、自宅PCで動作するオープンソースのOCRエンジンとマルチモーダルLLMを組み合わせて日本語レシートの読み取りを試みた実験を詳細に報告している。著者は合計15種類の設定（モデル・プロンプト・後処理の組み合わせ）と11枚の実際のレシートを用いてベンチマークを実施し、文字認識精度・処理速度・ハルシネーション率を定量的に評価した。結果として、小規模なVision-Language Model（VLM）単独では印刷品質の低いレシートや割引券の読み取りに失敗するケースが多く、OCRエンジンの前処理との組み合わせが精度向上に有効であることが示された。また、プロンプトに構造化出力の指定（JSON形式）を加えることで、後段のパース処理が格段に安定することも確認された。ローカルAIを実務に活用する際の具体的な指針として参考になる内容だ。

Một bài viết kỹ thuật đăng trên Zenn báo cáo chi tiết thí nghiệm kết hợp engine OCR mã nguồn mở chạy trên máy tính cá nhân với multimodal LLM để đọc hóa đơn tiếng Nhật. Ngoài ra, việc thêm chỉ định đầu ra có cấu trúc (định dạng JSON) vào prompt được xác nhận giúp quá trình parse ở bước tiếp theo ổn định hơn nhiều. Đây là nội dung tham khảo hữu ích như hướng dẫn cụ thể khi áp dụng AI cục bộ vào thực tế.

C1Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own Weights — New Research Shows Self-Modifying LLMs

📰 Article (English)

Researchers have published a paper on arXiv demonstrating that large language models can be trained to directly modify their own weight parameters during inference — a capability the authors term "weight rewriting." The technique works by training the model to generate delta weight updates as a form of output, which are then applied to the model's parameters in a controlled manner. The paper shows that models can use this capability to perform targeted self-correction on specific knowledge domains without full fine-tuning, and can "unlearn" specific information on demand. Experiments demonstrated that models could update factual knowledge, adjust behavioral tendencies, and even repair capability regressions introduced by prior fine-tuning. The approach remains experimental: weight updates must be constrained to prevent catastrophic forgetting, and the computational overhead is significant. However, the implications for agentic systems that need to adapt continuously without expensive retraining cycles are substantial, and the work has attracted significant discussion on Hacker News with over 230 upvotes.
