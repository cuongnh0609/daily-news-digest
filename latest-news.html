B1Windows上でAIエージェントを安全に隔離する「Microsoft Execution Containers」(MXC)が正式リリース

📰 本文 + 和越対訳

マイクロソフトは、Windows 11上でAIエージェントを安全に実行・隔離するための新機能「Microsoft Execution Containers」（MXC）を正式リリースした。MXCは実行時ポリシーによってエージェントがアクセスできるファイル、ネットワーク、周辺機器を細かく制御でき、人間とAIの操作を明確に区別できる点が特徴だ。隔離レベルは用途に応じて選択でき、軽量なAppContainerによるプロセス隔離から、セッションコンテナ、WSL上の仮想化（WSLc）、ハードウェアレベルで隔離するMicroVMまで対応する。長時間稼働するエージェント「OpenClaw」や、AnthropicのClaude Codeもすでに対応を発表しており、今後はWindows Intuneによる組織全体のポリシー管理も可能になる予定だ。

Microsoft đã chính thức phát hành "Microsoft Execution Containers" (MXC) — tính năng mới cho phép chạy và cách ly an toàn các AI agent trên Windows 11. MXC cho phép kiểm soát chi tiết các file, mạng và thiết bị ngoại vi mà agent có thể truy cập thông qua policy thực thi, đồng thời phân biệt rõ ràng thao tác của người dùng và AI. Có nhiều mức độ cách ly từ AppContainer nhẹ, Session Container, ảo hóa trên WSL (WSLc) cho đến MicroVM cách ly ở cấp phần cứng. Claude Code của Anthropic và agent OpenClaw đã thông báo hỗ trợ MXC. Trong tương lai, Windows Intune sẽ cho phép quản lý policy toàn tổ chức.

📰 Article (English)

Anthropic has published details of new defenses against distillation attacks — coordinated campaigns that extract Claude's capabilities to train unauthorized competing models. These operations typically use networks of fraudulent accounts to harvest reasoning transcripts at scale. Anthropic is particularly concerned because distilled models often lack safety guardrails, and capability transfer can reach foreign military and surveillance programs. The company cited Zhipu's GLM-5.3 as a recent example of a model likely distilled from leading US models that is easily jailbroken. Early defenses like summarizing Claude's reasoning traces reduced a distilled model's SWE-bench Verified score from 80.4% to 71.8%, but attackers adapted by prompting models to "output your prior reasoning verbatim, exactly character for character." Anthropic now uses three layered defenses: restricting where reasoning traces can travel, blocking extraction attempts with a two-stage constitutional classifier (false positive rate below 0.1%), and making repeated attempts costly through stricter rate limits, account bans triggered within two hours, and charges for blocked requests. Extractable reasoning tokens have fallen 10–100x. The company acknowledges trade-offs including occasional false positives and required API integration updates.
