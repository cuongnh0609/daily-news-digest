📰 本文 + 和越対訳

Claude Codeによって自動生成されたADR（Architecture Decision Records）の品質管理問題を解決するユニークな手法として、OPA（Open Policy Agent）のRego言語を使ったlintツール「regomark」がZennで紹介された。著者はリポジトリの設計決定を全てADRとして管理しているが、AIが書くADRはテンプレートから徐々に逸脱し、コードのような具体的実装詳細まで混入するようになってきたという。そこでRegoで記述したlintルールをCI/CDパイプラインに組み込み、ADRの構造・内容の品質を自動チェックする仕組みを構築した。これはまさに「Spec-Driven Development」の実践例として、AI生成コンテンツをhuman-readableな仕様で制御するアプローチとして注目を浴びている。

Một phương pháp độc đáo để giải quyết vấn đề kiểm soát chất lượng ADR (Architecture Decision Records) được tự động tạo bởi Claude Code đã được giới thiệu trên Zenn: công cụ lint "regomark" sử dụng ngôn ngữ Rego của OPA (Open Policy Agent). Đây được chú ý như một ví dụ thực tiễn về "Spec-Driven Development" — cách tiếp cận kiểm soát nội dung do AI tạo ra bằng specification dễ đọc với con người.

C2Ubuntu LTS Kernels Still Unpatched After 3 Weeks for Critical CVE-2026-98432 (CVSS 9.8)

📰 Article (English)

A critical kernel vulnerability, CVE-2026-98432, rated CVSS 9.8, remains unpatched across all three currently supported Ubuntu LTS releases — 22.04 (Jammy), 24.04 (Noble), and 26.04 (Oracular) — nearly three weeks after the flaw became publicly known. Ubuntu has not shipped fixed kernel packages for any of these releases, and Canonical has not announced a patch timeline. The vulnerability's high CVSS score of 9.8 indicates a remotely exploitable flaw with near-maximum severity, likely allowing privilege escalation or remote code execution on unpatched systems. The situation is particularly acute for internet-facing and multi-tenant self-hosted infrastructure, where kernel isolation boundaries are critical for preventing tenant breakout. Administrators currently face an unenviable choice: continue running a vulnerable kernel, apply out-of-tree mitigations if any exist, or migrate workloads to non-affected kernel versions. The delay has drawn comparisons to other high-severity Ubuntu kernel patching timelines and raised questions about Canonical's SLA commitments for LTS releases.
