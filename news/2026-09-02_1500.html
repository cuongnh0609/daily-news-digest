C2The Efficient Frontier of LLM Inference — A Framework for Latency-Throughput Optimization

📰 Article (English)

Baseten, an inference infrastructure company, published a comprehensive framework for thinking about LLM deployment optimization called "the efficient frontier." The core insight: inference techniques either move a deployment along the latency-throughput frontier (trading one for the other) or push the entire frontier outward (creating universal gains). Two categories of optimization strategies emerge. Tradeoff management techniques let engineers target specific operating points: batch sizing adjusts how many requests are processed concurrently; parallelism strategy distributes the model across GPUs via tensor parallelism or attention data parallelism; quantization reduces numerical precision to improve both speed and throughput at a minor quality cost. Frontier-pushing techniques create universal improvements: kernel optimization improves low-level computational efficiency; speculative decoding predicts and validates tokens in parallel to reduce latency; disaggregation separates the prefill and decode phases onto dedicated workers to maximize hardware utilization. The framework is practically actionable: a latency-sensitive application (chatbot) optimizes differently than a throughput-sensitive one (batch document processing). Understanding which dimension matters most for a given workload is the first step to deploying LLMs cost-effectively at scale.
