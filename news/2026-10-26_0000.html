The AAIF will host the Agent Skills name, logo, GitHub organization, and agentskills.io domain. Engineers from Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, and other companies will maintain the standard as individual contributors, with David Soria Parra and Mahesh Murag as Lead Maintainers. The roadmap includes versioned releases, a formal proposal process, and planned security guidance for skill authors and installers.
