C3Cloudflare Workers AI: How Quantization and Integrity Checks Serve Kimi and GLM at Scale

📰 Article (English)

Cloudflare's Workers AI team has published a detailed engineering post explaining how they serve large language models like Kimi and GLM efficiently across their global GPU fleet. The post covers three core techniques. First, KV cache quantization: reducing the key-value cache precision from 16-bit (FP16) to 8-bit integers cuts memory usage by 50% and doubles context capacity, with benchmarks showing 41% higher peak throughput compared to FP16 baselines. Second, weight quantization: compressing model weights from 8-bit to 4-bit integers reduces memory requirements by 40% and boosts decode speed by up to 55%. Third, integrity checking: since hundreds of concurrent inference requests share the same cached memory pages, Cloudflare implemented per-page checksums to detect memory corruption silently introduced by hardware errors. All three optimizations maintain model accuracy — the quantized models show no statistically significant quality degradation on standard benchmarks. The post is notable for combining practical GPU engineering with production reliability concerns, and for open-sourcing the benchmarking methodology.
