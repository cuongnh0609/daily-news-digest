📰 Article (English)

LangChain released Managed Deep Agents v0.10 on October 29, 2026, adding two major capabilities: full browser use and asynchronous subagent spawning. The feature is enabled through LangGraph's new async_spawn API surface, compatible with both Python and JavaScript SDKs. Pricing follows LangGraph Cloud's existing token-metered model.

C2AI Isn't Replacing SREs, It's Deskilling Them — A Growing Crisis in Platform Engineering

📰 Article (English)

A widely-shared piece by the SignOz engineering team, which topped Hacker News on October 30 with nearly 500 points, argues that the current wave of AI tooling in Site Reliability Engineering is not eliminating SRE roles — it is quietly degrading the engineering depth of the people who remain. The argument centers on a pattern the authors call "AI-mediated toil substitution": AI tools like incident summarizers, auto-remediation scripts, and AI-driven runbooks are handling the exact work that junior and mid-level SREs used to do to build intuition. When an AI surfaces a relevant runbook or auto-scales an impacted service before a human even pages, the SRE never develops the mental model of why the system was at risk in the first place. The piece draws a parallel to how GPS navigation reduced taxi drivers' spatial reasoning over time. Engineers who rely entirely on AI for on-call incident triage are accumulating less operational knowledge per incident. The piece cites internal data showing that teams using fully-automated remediation for six or more months struggle significantly more on novel incidents — failures the AI patterns haven't seen — compared to teams that kept humans in the loop for at least the diagnostic phase. The authors are not anti-AI; they propose a "deliberate practice" model where AI handles speed-critical tasks (alert deduplication, runbook retrieval) while humans own the diagnostic loop, even when AI assistance is available. The goal is to preserve the tacit knowledge that makes SREs valuable during black swan events.
